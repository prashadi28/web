01 / Research Domain

The Domain & Problem Context

Addressing the healthcare challenges of the rapidly ageing population through continuous physiological screening and edge-driven emergency response.

Literature Survey & Demographic Urgency

The World Health Organization (WHO) projects that by 2030, one in six people globally will be aged 60 years or over, expanding to over 2.1 billion by 2050. Sri Lanka represents one of the most rapidly ageing demographics in South Asia, where the percentage of senior citizens is steadily climbing while traditional joint-family caregiver structures evolve into nuclear units with adults away during working hours.

Elderly individuals living independently or in assisted living facilities face severe risks of unmonitored medical trauma. Acute conditions such as intermittent cardiac arrhythmias, silent hypoxia, thermal dysregulation leading to sepsis, and catastrophic falls occur suddenly without warning. Standard clinic check-ups only provide isolated snapshots, while commercial consumer wearables suffer from frequent false fall triggers and depend strictly on cloud connectivity that fails during rural network dropouts.

Core Research Problems Identified

Through comprehensive literature analysis and clinical consultation, three fundamental vulnerabilities in current senior monitoring systems were isolated:

Isolated Parameter Monitoring

Commercial wristbands track basic isolated metrics (such as daily steps or spot heart rate) in isolation. They lack compound multi-anomaly correlation required to predict serious systemic crises, such as cross-evaluating skin temperature with heart rate for sepsis deterioration, or correlating motion with galvanic skin conductance for distress.

High False-Alarm Rates in Fall Detection

Wrist-only accelerometers trigger constant false positives from routine upper-limb activities such as vigorous hand gestures, table banging, or forceful clapping. This causes severe "alarm fatigue" among family caregivers, leading them to ultimately disable or ignore critical safety alerts.

Absence of Emergency Dispatch Routing

Most existing health trackers rely on passive in-app smartphone push notifications that often go unread when caregivers are away. They fail to establish immediate, low-latency WebSocket routing and automated geolocation dispatch to emergency ambulance response units during life-threatening falls or collapses.

Primary Goal

Main Research Objective

To design, implement, and rigorously evaluate a privacy-centric, edge-computing dual-node IoT smart wearable system (SmartGlove) capable of continuous physiological screening, kinematic fall/immobility verification, and low-latency emergency dispatch routing via WebSockets and OpenStreetMap integration.

Zero Cloud Dependency for Fall Alerts Sub-1.5s Latency Privacy-Preserving Edge Inference
01

Cardiovascular Anomaly Screening

Acquire 250 Hz single-lead ECG via AD8232, extract 32 morphological features, and perform real-time arrhythmia screening trained on the MIT-BIH dataset.

02

Hip-Worn Fall & Immobility Verification

Deploy a dual-stage kinematic verification algorithm on ESP32-C3 correlating high-G impact with tilt angle and 3-8 Hz Parkinsonian tremor screening.

03

Respiratory & Thermal Deterioration

Implement continuous MAX30102 PPG hypoxemia scoring (<92%) and multi-vital sepsis risk prediction combining MLX90614 skin temperature with heart rate trends.

04

Multisensor Affective Stress Detection

Fuse Galvanic Skin Response (GSR), heart rate variability, gyroscope motion, and facial expressions using a WESAD-trained model to distinguish distress from exercise.

System Methodology

Multi-Layer System Architecture

End-to-end data flow: Hardware Sensors → Edge ESP32 Nodes → Local Hub ML Models → Master Risk Fusion → Caregiver & Ambulance Portals.

Enlarge Architecture
1. Sensing & Edge Layer

Dual microcontrollers (ESP32-S3 Mini on glove, ESP32-C3 Supermini on belt) capture physiological and kinematic telemetry with local digital filtering.

2. Inference & Fusion Layer

FastAPI local hub executes 5 ML pipelines, calculating individual anomaly probabilities and synthesizing the 0-100 Master Risk Index.

3. Telemetry & Dispatch Layer

Real-time WebSockets push alerts to family caregivers and stream live GPS coordinates directly to emergency medical ambulance dashboards.

The Four Core Research Subsystems

Cardiovascular Anomaly & Arrhythmia Screening

Continuous beat-level arrhythmia screening using 250 Hz AD8232 single-lead ECG coupled with MLX90614 infrared temperature sensor to detect cardiac rhythm irregularities and heat-stress before visible collapse occurs.

Dataset:MIT-BIH Arrhythmia
Features:32 Morphological Peaks
Emergency Trigger:Arrhythmia Risk ≥85%

Component 02

Kinematic Fall & Immobility Detection (Smart Belt)

Lakshan N.K.T. (IT22196156)

A hip-worn smart belt isolating center-of-mass kinematics. Employs a dual-stage algorithm combining vector impact magnitude (>24 m/s²) with post-impact tilt angle (>55°) and 3-8 Hz Parkinsonian tremor screening.

Sensor:MPU6050 6-Axis IMU
Node MCU:ESP32-C3 Supermini
Dataset:SisFall & KFall
Verification:Impact + 1.5s Window Tilt
Special Feature:3-8 Hz Tremor Band Power
View Component Thesis
Component 03

Respiratory, Hypoxemia & Thermal Warning System

Dissanayaka D.M.S.K. (IT22291196)

Early detection of silent hypoxemia (SpO2 < 92%) via MAX30102 pulse oximeter raw PPG signals, correlated with MLX90614 temperature and heart rate trends to detect early systemic inflammatory sepsis onset.

Sensors:MAX30102 + MLX90614
Hypoxemia ML:Logistic Regression (BIDMC)
Sepsis ML:Random Forest (PhysioNet ICU)
Vital Fusion:Temp + HR + SpO2 Trends
Alert Threshold:SpO2 < 85% / High SIRS
View Component Thesis
Component 04

Stress Detection & Affective Computing System

Edirisinghe A.P. Peshala (IT22146410)

Quantifying silent emotional distress and unspoken pain in non-verbal elderly individuals via Galvanic Skin Response (GSR), skin temperature, and motion fusion, distinguishing psychological distress from physical exercise.

Sensors:GSR + MPU6050 + MAX30102
ML Model:Random Forest (WESAD)
Fusion Formula:0.65×ML + 0.20×GSR + 0.15×Gyro
Vision Engine:Facial Stress Screening
Scoring Range:0 - 100% Stress Likelihood
